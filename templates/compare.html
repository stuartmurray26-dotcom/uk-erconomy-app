Key Observations

  • The 1990–1992 period saw very high bank rates (above 10%) used to control runaway inflation and defend Sterling in the ERM.
  • The 2009 financial crisis led to emergency cuts to a then-historic low of 0.5%, maintained for seven years.
  • The 2022–2023 cost-of-living crisis pushed CPI to its highest level since the early 1990s, prompting rapid rate rises from 0.1% to 5.25%.
  • Years with a positive real rate rewarded savers; years with a negative real rate eroded savings.
